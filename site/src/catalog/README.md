# Device and scenario catalog

`devices.ts` is the typed source of truth for 49 fixed choices in four groups: seven conventional weapons, 34 nuclear devices, three MIRV systems, and five hypothetical studies. IDs, names, displayed yields, and internal megaton yields remain stable for retained entries. The conventional group is ordered by ascending TNT-equivalent yield; other groups retain prototype order. `selectableYield.ts` defines the inclusive 1.5 Tt (1,500,000 Mt) product ceiling for each fixed or custom choice. Above-ceiling entries and their dependent scenarios are absent rather than remapped to smaller devices. The added conventional estimates use specified explosive fills and approximate peak-pressure equivalence, with assumptions in [model notes](../../docs/model.md). MIRV entries distinguish aggregate display yield from per-warhead yield; `perWarheadYieldMt` returns the latter for one impact. `getDeviceById` returns `undefined` for unknown or removed IDs, and `isConventionalDevice` identifies deliveries that receive a conventional plume and no nuclear Fallout footprint.

Custom yield is an application input and is not counted among the 49 fixed devices. `scenarios.ts` contains 16 retained labels, categories, device mappings, and coordinates in degrees: 11 historical and five hypothetical scenarios. `getScenarioById` returns an entry or `undefined`. Catalog tests protect their values, the approved conventional ordering, the ceiling, and the absence of stale removed IDs.

`previewTypes.ts` defines display-only dossiers and scenario records. `deviceDossiers.ts` supplies exterior facts, confidence, per-field source URLs, entity-specific field labels, qualified outlines, and research notes for every fixed device; `scenarioPreviews.ts` pairs each scenario with static artwork and a concise researched event description of 30–45 words. These modules load with the desktop preview and do not feed physics calculations. Estimated facts are marked **(est)**, reported claims remain qualified, and unknown or inapplicable fields are explicit in the data. The facts-only weapon panel omits optional inapplicable payload/fill rows and keeps research notes and provenance in the documentation. The audited SVGs use recognizable public exterior proportions with no scale text or reference bar; LENGTH remains a fact row. Some views, including JASSM’s estimated plan view, are named explicitly rather than implied to be side elevations. One-off test apparatus, MIRV carriers, and hypothetical studies are identified so an illustration cannot imply a documented operational weapon. Davy Crockett depicts the complete M388 projectile, with its component warhead identified separately. The custom preview states that no physical weapon is specified.

The [research ledger](../../docs/catalog-research.md) records sources, conversions, variant ambiguities, and limits. [Artwork notes](../../docs/preview-artwork.md) describe the generated illustrations and prompt set. Neither imagery nor event prose is a forecast or archival photograph.
